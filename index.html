Benchmark · v2.0

One task. Three renderings. Nowhere to hide.

Every problem in MultiNet v2.0 is the same underlying maze — with hidden causal mechanisms a model must discover to solve it. We render that maze across three interfaces. A model that truly understands the task should solve all three; a model leaning on a memorized format solves one and collapses on the rest.

Domain 01

GridWorld

The maze as a symbolic grid of cells, walls, and switches. Pure spatial reasoning — no rendering, no language, just the structure of the problem.

Domain 02

Natural Language

The same maze described in text. The model must build and hold a spatial model of the world from a narrative it reads step by step.

Domain 03

3D

The maze as a first-person 3D environment. Perception, navigation, and the same causal mechanics — now grounded in pixels and movement.

Release 1 focuses on GridWorld — the cleanest place to establish the methodology, difficulty axes, and evaluation protocol. Natural language and 3D follow, culminating in the full three-domain benchmark and generator.
