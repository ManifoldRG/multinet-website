Frontier models struggle on 2D mazes.

The first release of MultiNet v2.0 drops models into a 2D maze with walls, keys, doors, switches, gates, distractors, and a goal tile. There are six valid actions and the mechanisms are not explained to the models. 3 frontier models solve 6 out of 150 episodes.

    Why mazes with mechanisms

    Measuring long-horizon action needs a task with four properties at once. This is the simplest structure we know of that has all four.

    Knowledge-free

    Nothing to know. No API, no jargon, no priors — so failure cannot be blamed on unfamiliarity.

    Generative

    No fixed answer key to leak into training data, and difficulty that scales instead of saturating.

    Projectable

    The same maze re-renders in language or 3D, turning generalization into a direct comparison.

    Verifiable

    Breadth-first search gives the exact optimal path from any state, so a single move can be called wrong.

    How the models did

    Three frontier models, 50 mazes, three attempts each. Solves alone cannot separate them, so progress toward the goal and episode length are reported alongside.
